# Atlas Product Vision

## Mission

Atlas exists to preserve the long-term story of a person's health by transforming coaching conversations into a trusted, structured, and enduring health memory.

---

## Vision

Atlas is a Personal Health Memory System.

It provides a permanent record of meaningful health observations, coaching outcomes, and progress toward long-term goals.

Rather than replacing coaching, Atlas preserves it.

Every meaningful health conversation contributes to a lifelong health record that helps people make better decisions over time.

---

## The Problem

Health coaching is conversational and dynamic.

Health progress is measured over months and years.

Today's coaching conversations often produce valuable insights, but those insights are rarely preserved in a structured way that supports long-term reflection and analysis.

Atlas bridges that gap.

It transforms the outcome of coaching into a permanent health memory that can be reviewed, visualized, and used to support future coaching conversations.

---

## Relationship with ChatGPT

Atlas and ChatGPT have complementary responsibilities.

ChatGPT provides coaching, reasoning, education, and conversation.

Atlas preserves the outcomes of those conversations.

The two systems are intentionally independent.

ChatGPT can function without Atlas.

Atlas can preserve observations produced by any coaching system that follows the Atlas Observation Contract.

Together they create a complete long-term health management experience.

---

## Product Principles

Atlas is guided by the following principles.

### 1. Atlas is a Personal Health Memory System.

Its primary responsibility is to preserve meaningful health observations over time.

### 2. Atlas is not a coach.

Coaching, reasoning, education, and conversation belong to ChatGPT.

### 3. Atlas preserves outcomes, not conversations.

Atlas stores finalized observations rather than complete coaching sessions.

### 4. Simplicity over features.

Every feature must solve a real user problem.

Features that add complexity without improving long-term health understanding should not be added.

### 5. The user owns their health history.

Observations remain portable and exportable.

Atlas never locks users into a proprietary format.

### 6. Long-term trends are more valuable than daily fluctuations.

Atlas emphasizes sustained progress toward long-term health goals rather than short-term changes.

### 7. Atlas complements ChatGPT.

Atlas exists because coaching is dynamic while health history is permanent.

Neither system replaces the other.

Together they provide a complete health management experience.

---

## Evolution

Atlas is designed so that the user experience can improve without changing the relationship between Atlas and ChatGPT.

Version 1 exchanges information through manual import and export.

Future versions may support secure, automated synchronization when technology allows.

The architectural responsibilities remain unchanged:

- ChatGPT coaches.
- Atlas preserves.

---

## Atlas Version 1 Success Criteria

Atlas Version 1 is successful when:

- A weekly coaching conversation naturally concludes with the creation of an Atlas Observation.

- Atlas accurately preserves those observations as a permanent health memory.

- Users can import observations without manual editing.

- Users can view their current health dashboard at any time.

- Users can export their complete health history for longitudinal analysis in ChatGPT.

- Atlas remains intentionally simple and focused on preserving health history rather than providing coaching.

---

## Version 1 Non-Goals

Atlas Version 1 will not:

- Replace ChatGPT as a coaching system.

- Store complete coaching conversations.

- Perform independent health analysis.

- Require automated synchronization between Atlas and ChatGPT.

- Introduce features that do not improve the long-term health record.

Future versions may expand Atlas, but only if they remain consistent with the Product Principles.

---

## Definition of Success

Years from now, a user should be able to look back across hundreds of health observations and understand not only what happened, but why.

Atlas should preserve the story of a person's health journey in a way that no individual coaching conversation, wearable device, or health application can accomplish on its own.

ChatGPT helps the user improve this week.

Atlas helps the user understand their journey.
