# Atlas Observation Contract

Every Atlas Observation contains three sections.

## Section 1 — Observations

Raw data collected during the check-in.

Examples:

- Weight
- Recovery
- Activity
- Nutrition
- Alcohol
- Context
- Monthly metrics (when applicable)

---

## Section 2 — Analysis

Generated by Wilson.

Examples:

- Adaptive TDEE
- Average Daily Deficit
- Weekly Strength Volume
- Average Strength Volume per Session
- Overall Status
- Confidence
- Confidence Reason

---

## Section 3 — Coaching

Generated by Wilson.

Includes:

- Biggest Win
- Biggest Opportunity
- Focus Next Week
- Coach Summary

---

## Contract

Atlas stores every observation exactly as Wilson produced it.

Atlas never recalculates historical observations.

Atlas presents observations through dashboards and trends.

Wilson is solely responsible for analysis and coaching.

Atlas observations are immutable. Historical observations are never modified after creation. Improvements in Wilson affect only future Observations.
