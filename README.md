# Atlas v1

Atlas is a personal health intelligence system that transforms structured health observations into a weekly executive dashboard.

## Purpose

Atlas exists to generate a clear, accurate, and actionable picture of health over time.

The primary output of Atlas is the **Weekly Snapshot Dashboard**.

ChatGPT remains the primary interface for health conversations, coaching, and longitudinal analysis.

## Responsibilities

Atlas is responsible for:

- Maintaining the personal health journal
- Producing the Weekly Snapshot Dashboard
- Calculating trends and historical metrics
- Supporting DEXA scans, laboratory results, and annual physicals
- Calculating the Atlas Health Score (under development)

## Design Philosophy

Atlas has one primary interface:

**The Weekly Snapshot Dashboard.**

The dashboard provides a concise executive summary of health.

ChatGPT provides the interpretation, coaching, and recommendations.
